Snug privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Snug is made by Snugworks.

Snug is a game for everyone, including children. We designed it to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Snug has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to send messages or photos. We don't ask for your name, email, age or location.

What stays on your device

Your best score, coins, skins, hats, settings and daily progress are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in a browser) deletes them.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app shows a small number of ads from Google AdMob: optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward, and an occasional short ad between games. The web version has no ads.

  • Every ad request is marked as directed to children, so Google applies its rules for children's apps.
  • Ads are not personalised, and are limited to content suitable for all ages.
  • The app does not use the device's advertising ID.
  • To show an ad, Google receives basic technical information such as the device type and IP address. Google's handling of this is described in how Google uses information in ads.

Sharing a challenge

After a game you can share a challenge link with a friend. A grown-up must answer a question first. The link contains only the game's number and your score, nothing about you. Sharing uses your phone's own share menu, and we don't see who you send it to.

Purchases

The Android app offers two optional one-time purchases: Snug Plus (no ads between games, every skin and hat in the shop, and the Golden blob) and Remove Ads. They are cosmetic and comfort only and never change how the game plays. A grown-up must answer a question before any purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see payment details, and the app only asks Google Play which purchases your account owns.

Play statistics

Snug keeps simple play counts on your own device, such as how many games were played and how long they lasted, to help us test and tune the game. They are never sent anywhere. A hidden tester screen can show them and copy them as text, which is only shared if you choose to send it yourself.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or under 18 in India). If you think something was collected, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
