Snug privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · Snug is made by Snugworks.

Snug is a game for everyone, including children. We designed it to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Snug has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to send messages or photos. We don't ask for your name, email, age or location.

What stays on your device

Your best score, coins, skins, hats, stickers, settings, daily progress, the levels you draw and the secret codes you type (with your best score and best run's "ghost" for each) are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in a browser) deletes them.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app shows a small number of ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch to double the coins from a game, and an occasional short ad between games. Second chances in a game never need an ad. The web version has no ads.

  • Every ad request is marked as directed to children, so Google applies its rules for children's apps.
  • Ads are not personalised, and are limited to content suitable for all ages.
  • The app does not use the device's advertising ID.
  • To show an ad, Google receives basic technical information such as the device type and IP address. Google's handling of this is described in how Google uses information in ads.

Sharing a challenge

After a game you can share a challenge link with a friend, together with a picture of your result (your blob, score and medal). A grown-up must answer a question first. The link contains only the game's number, your score and your blob's size along the way (so your friend can race your "ghost"), nothing about you. The picture is made on your device, sharing uses your phone's own share menu, and we don't see who you send it to.

Levels you draw become a short code made only of the gate sizes. Sending one to a friend works the same way and also needs a grown-up.

Hum to Grow (web version only)

If a grown-up turns on Hum to grow in Settings, the web version uses the device's microphone during games to tell whether you are humming. The sound is checked on your device as you play and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can turn it off at any time in Settings, which stops the microphone. The Android app doesn't use the microphone at all.

Purchases

Snug has no purchases. Everything in the game is earned by playing.

Play statistics

Snug keeps simple play counts on your own device, such as how many games were played and how long they lasted, to help us test and tune the game. They are never sent anywhere. A hidden tester screen can show them and copy them as text, which is only shared if you choose to send it yourself.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or under 18 in India). If you think something was collected, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
